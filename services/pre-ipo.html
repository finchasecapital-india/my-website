What it is

Plain language, then the law.

Group cleanup, related parties and restatements before the DRHP, a quarter-by-quarter plan, not a tracker.

Exit-phase work treats the filing as a lagging indicator — the three years of decisions before it are the actual product. KPIs defined, computed, and assured the same way across the DRHP save a comment cycle; changing a definition mid-process costs one.

The output is not a PDF dump. It is a file a future investor, banker, or officer can reopen without you in the room.

What you walk away withA quarter-by-quarter clean-up plan.Banker handoff ready.Filed, audited, funded without leaving the building.
When to call usListing is 12–24 months out.Two clean comparatives needed.
Our process
  1. 01

    Audit

    Group structure, RPTs, policies and KMP gaps scored against listing norms.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Quarter-by-quarter cleanup plan with owners and dates.

  3. 03

    Restate

    Prior periods restated; policies rewritten for scrutiny.

  4. 04

    Govern

    Board composition, committees and KMP readiness closed out.

  5. 05

    Hand
