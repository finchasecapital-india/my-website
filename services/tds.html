What it is

Plain language, then the law.

Deduction logic mapped per payment type, deposits on time, 24Q/26Q filed, no interest leakage.

Operate-phase work is what keeps every later phase honest — a bank, an auditor, or an acquirer forms their first opinion of you from the same ledger you file from every month. A return filed on time and a return that reconciles to the books are two different achievements — we treat both as the job.

The output is not a PDF dump. It is a file a future investor, banker, or officer can reopen without you in the room.

What you walk away withA deduction map with filed returns.No interest leakage, demands resolved.Filed, audited, funded without leaving the building.
When to call usMany vendors and uncertain sections.Sections mapped per payment type.
Our process
  1. 01

    Mapping

    Every payment type mapped to its section: 194C, 194J, 192 and the rest.

  2. 02

    Logic

    Rate, threshold and lower-deduction certificates applied per vendor.

  3. 03

    Deposits

    Monthly TDS deposited on time; interest leakage killed at source.

  4. 04

    Returns

    24Q/26Q filed quarterly with challan matching.

  5. 05

    Closure

    Corrections, demand resolution and Form 16/16A issuance.
