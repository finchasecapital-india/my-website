What it is

Plain language, then the law.

Capital Restructuring and Financial Reengineering covers bonus issues, buybacks, mergers, and debt-equity conversions that need to be registrable with the MCA and defensible to every shareholder in the room. Finchase designs the structure and carries it through filing.

We start from your current cap table and balance sheet, then model the restructuring against tax, control, and lender consent — not just the accounting entries.

The output is not a scheme document alone. It is a structure that survives ROC scrutiny and shareholder questions.

Why it matters

Time

Tribunal and ROC timelines do not compress for a late start. A structure filed in a hurry invites resubmission.

Diligence

Acquirers and lenders read restructuring history closely. A clean scheme is easier to underwrite than one with unresolved minority objections.

Decisions

Bonus vs buyback vs merger changes control, tax, and dilution differently. That choice deserves a model, not a template.
