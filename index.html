Process
How we work

From your idea to a locked mandate — in 72 hours, not 72 days.

No back-and-forth, no waiting weeks for a proposal. A clear process, a clear clock.

01

You bring us the idea

A conversation, a call, or a submission — however it reaches us, the clock starts here. Within 24 hours, we send back exactly what we need from you to move forward.

02

Idea screening

We assess the idea on its own terms — what it's solving, for whom, and what shape of business it needs to become.

03

The numbers

We stress-test the basic economics — what it costs to build, what it needs to earn, and where the real risk sits.

04

Market scenario analysis

We map the idea against the market it's actually entering — not the market it wishes it was entering.

05

Our proposal

One clear proposal — scope, fees, and approach in writing. No surprise line items later.

06

Mandate locked, work begins

We negotiate what needs negotiating, finalize the mandate, and start work — all inside 48 hours of receiving your complete documents.
